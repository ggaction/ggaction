# Third-party dataset notices

The optional real-data corpus comes from the
[TidyTuesday repository](https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday) at the
exact commit recorded in `manifest.json`. TidyTuesday is organized by the Data
Science Learning Community and its repository is distributed under CC0-1.0.

TidyTuesday aggregates data from other sources. Its license disclaims
responsibility for clearing third-party rights. ggaction therefore keeps raw
downloads in the gitignored `.artifacts/` directory and records the original
repository path and immutable digest for reproducible local testing.

The initial corpus uses the following TidyTuesday weeks:

- Palmer penguins, 2020-07-28
- Himalayan climbing expeditions, 2020-09-22
- London Marathon winners, 2023-04-25
- United States tornadoes, 2023-05-16
- Global surface temperatures, 2023-07-11

These files are test inputs only and are not included in the published npm
package.
