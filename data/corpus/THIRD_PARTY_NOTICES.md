# Third-party dataset notices

The optional real-data corpus references 50 immutable CSV blobs from the
[TidyTuesday repository](https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday) at commit
`1f5a20eae51d871ec4ac0f95d16e43b9ba3f1dec`. The TidyTuesday repository is CC0-1.0, but
the repository aggregates third-party data and does not clear every upstream
right. Raw files stay in the gitignored local artifact cache and are not
published with ggaction.

A license of **not stated** means the pinned TidyTuesday README did not state
a source-data license. It does not mean that the data is public domain or free
of restrictions. Follow the linked upstream terms before redistributing it.
